Erhän – Bespoke Furniture Platform

  • Category: UX/UI Design / Marketplace / E-commerce
  • Tools: Figma, Design Systems, Prototyping, User Flows, Responsive Design

Motivation: Commissioning bespoke furniture can be frustrating for both customers and craftsmen. Many existing platforms focus on finished products rather than the collaborative process of creating custom furniture, making communication, trust, and personalization difficult. I wanted to explore how a digital platform could make bespoke furniture commissioning more transparent, collaborative, and inspiring.

Overview: Erhän is a conceptual marketplace that connects customers directly with skilled furniture craftsmen. Rather than simply browsing products, users can discover artisans, communicate their ideas, build inspiration boards, request custom designs, and track the progress of their furniture from concept to completion.

Design Process: I mapped the end-to-end customer journey, identified common pain points within bespoke purchasing, and designed intuitive user flows focused on discovery, customization, communication, and trust. The interface emphasizes clean navigation, premium aesthetics, and a seamless experience across desktop and mobile devices.

Solution: Key features include artisan discovery, inspiration boards, personalized recommendations, project consultations, secure messaging, order tracking, and a streamlined checkout experience. The design balances the emotional experience of commissioning handcrafted furniture with the practical needs of managing custom orders.

Outcome: Erhän demonstrates my approach to solving complex user problems through product thinking, user-centered interface design, responsive layouts, and scalable design systems. The project showcases my ability to transform an idea into a cohesive digital product experience while maintaining a strong focus on usability and visual storytelling.
