Alùbara – Androgynous Fashion Platform

  • Category: UX/UI Design / Fashion E-commerce
  • Tools: Figma, Design Systems, Responsive Design, Prototyping, Branding

Motivation: Traditional fashion e-commerce platforms typically organize clothing by gender, making it difficult for people who prefer androgynous or gender-fluid fashion to browse naturally. I wanted to explore a shopping experience that prioritizes personal style over gender labels.

Overview: Alùbara is a conceptual fashion marketplace designed around identity, self-expression, and inclusive shopping. Instead of separating products into men's and women's collections, the platform organizes clothing by aesthetic, allowing users to discover pieces that match their personal style rather than predefined categories.

Design Process: The experience was designed around reducing friction during product discovery. I developed a visual identity, responsive layouts, reusable UI components, and mobile-first shopping flows that maintain consistency across desktop and mobile platforms. Particular attention was given to navigation, category organization, and creating a premium editorial shopping experience.

Solution: Alùbara introduces style-based browsing, curated collections, editorial content, responsive product discovery, and a cohesive design system that supports an inclusive shopping journey. Users can explore fashion through aesthetics such as Casual, Technical, Minimal, or Classic rather than traditional gender categories.

Outcome: This project demonstrates my ability to combine branding, interface design, responsive web design, and product thinking into a cohesive digital experience. It showcases my approach to designing accessible, visually engaging e-commerce products that challenge conventional shopping patterns while remaining intuitive and user-focused.
